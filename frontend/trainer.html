Teach SIGNOVA your signs

The existing model sometimes confuses signs. Record labelled examples of how you perform each phrase, then fine-tune the model on those examples. This uses the same 45-frame landmark format as recognition; you don't need to edit Python files.

For useful results: aim for 30–50 samples per sign (at least 15 each before training). Record each sign as a separate 3-second clip. Keep your face/upper body and both hands visible, and vary your position and lighting slightly. Perform the intended sign consistently. Training improves personalization but cannot guarantee perfect recognition.

1. Record a sample

Camera preview appears here
Start the camera, select a label, then record one complete sign.
Each sample is processed locally on your computer by the SIGNOVA backend. The saved dataset stays in this project folder.

2. Sample collection

Record examples for all three labels. More varied examples usually work better than repeating the exact same movement.

3. Train the personalized model

The training step starts from the existing checkpoint and fine-tunes its final layers. It may take a few minutes. Keep this browser tab open.
